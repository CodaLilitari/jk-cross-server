# Lily: learning from autonomous recovery outcomes

## Question and decision

Recovery v3 learns successful native corrections. This experiment asks whether a
neural controller initialized from v3 can improve by selecting its own successful
and failed physical attempts. It is a bounded, elitist evolutionary search over
the network's output head. It is not another imitation fit, PPO, live multiplayer
training, or a claim about general game strength.

The existing synchronous stock-input fixture already supplies exact autonomous
control and a dry-landing oracle. Use it unchanged. V3's observed failures include
surface contact, target-sector entry and near-goal trajectories, so none of those
proxies is a reward. Only a completed supported-dry landing counts as a win.

The user delegated the approach and repeatedly authorized continued implementation
and publication. Continue in the existing isolated feature worktree, with fresh
implementers and independent task reviews. Preserve the foundation branch, draft
PR stack and all existing model and fixture bytes.

## Cases and fixed budget

Before any new controller rollout, publish the exact three TSVs and their
geometry-only receipts under `qa/lily-outcome/`, generated by
`scripts/bot_development/analysis/declare_outcome_poses.py`. The generator uses
seed string `41024`, disjoint spatial cells, binary32 centers, radius .065,
conservative sector-plane clearance, historical-position exclusion and explicit
separation bounds. It never calls a policy or admits a case by observed success.

Training has 12 pool and four shaft groups, each at -90, 0, 90 and 180 degrees:
64 assignments. Development has six pool and two shaft groups at those headings:
32 assignments. Sealed testing has eight pool and four shaft groups at -45, 45,
135 and -135 degrees: 48 assignments. Sibling headings stay in the same split.
The broader pool region and shifted test headings are a deliberate joint transfer
test on one map and two known bank edges. They do not isolate position effects
from heading effects, or establish generalization to another map.

The declaration contains actual TSV digests, all assignment IDs and the eight
training batches. Construct those batches with NumPy `PCG64(31023)`: permute
the 48 pool IDs in TSV/yaw order, then the 16 shaft IDs; generation g receives
pool slice `[6*g:6*g+6]` followed by shaft slice `[2*g:2*g+2]`. Each assignment
appears in one batch. Controller execution order cannot affect randomness.

Each of eight generations evaluates its incumbent and six positive/negative
perturbation pairs on the same eight starts: 13 * 8 * 8 = 832 model executions.
Do not adapt this budget, batches or scales to observed outcomes. Evaluate all
three development checkpoints (v3, generation 4, generation 8), 96 executions,
even when weights coincide. If selection succeeds, run three controllers on
48 sealed starts (144 executions) and paired v3/selected on all 67 historical
diagnostic starts (134 executions). Maximum total: 1,206 executions. If no
candidate passes development, close after 928 and leave the sealed set unopened.

## Search and selection

Draw the full direction tensor from
`numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(41023)).standard_normal((8,6,6,33))`.
For generations 1-4, scale each coefficient of the first four output rows by
`.18 / sqrt(33)` and each button row by `.65 / sqrt(33)`; halve both scales in
generations 5-8. Pair plus/minus directions around the current incumbent.

Candidate order is incumbent, direction 0 positive, direction 0 negative,
through direction 5 negative. IDs are `g01-c00` through `g08-c12`. Incumbents
reuse their exact model bytes; proposals replace only head values and provenance.
Proposal provenance records the original v3 identity, actual parent canonical
digest, declaration digest, generation, direction, sign, seed and scales. Do not
inherit v3's supervised-fit provenance or append results to a measured model.

Rank a complete eight-start batch lexicographically by `(wins, -charged_steps)`.
`charged_steps` sums actual physics steps for wins and 9,000 for each loss.
Thus neither early death nor proximity can offset a missed landing. Keep the
first candidate on exact ties; an unchanged incumbent or all-failure generation
is valid. Retain every candidate, result and rejected proposal. This is an
elitist search, not the gradient-estimation algorithm in the 2017 ES paper.

On development, a candidate is eligible only if it preserves every shaft
assignment won by v3. Select the eligible candidate with the most pool wins,
strictly exceeding v3's pool count. Prefer generation 4 over generation 8 on an
exact count tie. Otherwise select none and stop. Freeze the chosen measured
model/export and a selection receipt before opening any sealed outcome.

The engineering transfer criterion is all of: complete paired evidence; at
least four net additional sealed pool wins; positive net improvement at three
or more sealed pool positions; zero lost sealed shaft assignments won by v3;
and zero losses among v3's 24 original shaft winners in the diagnostic rerun.
Report the paired gains, regressions and both/neither successes by pose. This
criterion is not a statistical significance test. No second candidate is chosen
after sealed results. Report other historical regressions even when outside the
criterion. Native is a separately measured reference, never an optimizer input.

## Execution and evidence

Build once in a new private run directory on `/dev/shm`; retain the executable,
numeric geometry and build inputs. Require adequate free space before training
(at least 2.5 GiB) and check remaining space between blocks. Keep uncompressed
raw evidence for this bounded run; do not add an archive/extraction subsystem.
Final raw size and available space must be reported. Owned geometry stays private.

Use the existing pinned `_execute_trial(..., None, status)` for model runs;
its public wrapper drops pure-run status. A small native-only Popen/wait/kill/reap
launcher records actual status too. Persist `process.json` in `finally`, including
actual engine and worker wait results, timeout/error, monotonic elapsed duration
and UTC start/end. Never manufacture a handoff to obtain process status.

Use the canonical private run directory `/dev/shm/<run_id>` from the declaration;
reject an alternate output path that could reset a partially consumed run. Use
exclusive directories and prewritten assignment claims for attempts. A stage
is not resumable: refuse an existing stage or ambiguous prior attempt. Do not
silently retry failed physical runs or launch a replacement block. Allow already
dispatched work to finish and preserve it when another attempt fails. Pending
assignments remain explicitly unrun. A fresh output directory is a distinct run,
not permission to omit a failed attempt from this declared experiment.

Every successful block gates on actual clean process status, engine log marker,
`motor.assess` with the observed code, and full frozen-export decision reinference.
Preserve every available byte on censoring. A censor stops scoring; malformed or
contradictory evidence raises an audit error. Compare paired initial observations,
seed, origin witness and target, allowing different first actions. Also bind the
seed/origin/target to the historical stock edge witness; agreement between wrong
starts is insufficient. Do not use legacy `validate_pairs`, which demands equal
first actions across models.

Receipts bind the declaration, source files, original fixture inventory, model
file/canonical/export digests, exact case assignments, process witnesses and raw
file hashes/sizes. A new independent audit reconstructs proposals, ranking,
generation lineage, development selection, freeze and final comparison from
the retained evidence without fitting or executing the game. Re-audit every
applied model decision. Separate assigned, attempted, completed, failed, censored
and unrun counts. No final result may be inferred from an unfinished run.

## Limits and continuation

This experiment can alter responses to existing observations but cannot add
memory, terrain perception, opponents or Force usage. It measures whether direct
outcome selection transfers beyond training starts. A negative outcome remains
publishable evidence and leaves v3 as the retained checkpoint. A positive result
still needs sensor parity and a separate live-game assessment. Later recurrence
or actor-critic work needs its own observation/state and stochastic-action
contracts; do not quietly add those to this experiment.

Background: Salimans et al., *Evolution Strategies as a Scalable Alternative to
Reinforcement Learning* (2017), https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03864. It motivates
black-box neural policy optimization; this small elitist recipe is our own
predeclared experiment, with no claim to reproduce that paper's results.
